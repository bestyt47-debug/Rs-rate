how the leaderboard works

Every time someone rates a username and chooses to save it, that score gets added to the pool the leaderboard pulls from. The ranking is a straight sort on the overall 0–100 score — no popularity votes, no manual curation — so a username's spot is earned purely by how it scores, not by how many likes it gets.

Because it's built from real scores run by real people trying out real handles, the leaderboard shifts over time as new usernames come in and old ones get pushed down. It's less a fixed hall of fame and more a live snapshot of what's currently scoring well.

why check the leaderboard

It's a quick way to calibrate your expectations before you rate your own username — seeing what a genuinely top-tier score looks like in practice is more useful than just staring at a number in isolation.

It's also a decent source of inspiration if you're stuck: scrolling the top entries can surface naming patterns and structures that tend to score well, which you can riff on for your own handle instead of starting from a blank page.

what the top usernames tend to have in common

Scroll through the current top of the board and a few patterns repeat: most are short, almost none lean on a string of numbers, and very few use an underscore or period to pad out an otherwise-taken word.

The highest scorers also tend to commit to one clear identity instead of mashing two unrelated words together — a gaming handle that sounds like a gaming handle, not a gaming word bolted onto a real name. That consistency is exactly what the vibe and aura categories are built to reward.

If you’re chasing a top spot, the fastest lever to pull usually isn’t originality — it’s cutting anything that doesn’t need to be there. Shorter, cleaner versions of your current username tend to outscore the padded original more often than a completely new idea does.

frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to appear on the leaderboard?
Yes — rating a username is account-free, but saving it so it's eligible for the leaderboard requires signing up, otherwise there's no record to rank.

How often does the leaderboard update?
It reflects the current saved scores in real time, so new high scorers can appear — or knock existing entries down — as soon as they're saved.

Can I remove my username from the leaderboard later?
Yes, if you'd rather not have a saved username publicly ranked, you can remove it from your saves and it drops out of the ranking.
