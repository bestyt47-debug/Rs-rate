Behind the score

How scoring works.

Rate My Username runs a rule-based calculation in your browser. It does not use an AI model, search social accounts, check availability, or measure your popularity. The labels are for entertainment and can help you compare text variations.

The actual formula

Each category is scored from 1 to 100. The overall score is rounded from 0.27 × Vibe + 0.25 × Originality + 0.28 × Aura + 0.20 × Memorability. Categories are not four equal 25-point blocks.

  • Vibe: vowel balance, length and fixed pattern bonuses.
  • Originality: unique letter/digit ratio, number use, separator count and pattern bonuses. This does not compare your name to other users.
  • Aura: repeated-character runs, separators, length and pattern bonuses.
  • Memorability: 40% length signal, 30% repeated-character signal and 30% vowel-balance signal, plus a small seeded adjustment.

Why results repeat

The tool trims surrounding spaces and leading @ signs. A lowercase version seeds a small adjustment for each category, so the same input repeats its score. Changing characters can change both the structural signals and that adjustment. Roast lines use a separate random selection and can vary.

A practical comparison

In the current code, lumen scores 83/100 and lumen2026 scores 79/100. The second adds four digits to nine characters and a trailing year-like pattern, changing the number-use signal. It does not prove the first name is available or that strangers will prefer it. Try saying both aloud, ask a friend to type them, and check the platform before deciding.

For mira (74/100) versus mira.studio (88/100), the second can communicate a creative purpose as well as changing the score. The algorithm cannot understand that purpose; your judgment is part of the decision.

Known limitations

  • The length signal favors 5–16 characters; the shortest name is not automatically best.
  • One or two dots/underscores can score higher than none. Do not add punctuation only to chase points.
  • Vowel and letter checks focus on English A–Z. Names in other scripts can receive misleading results.
  • Pattern bonuses and seeded adjustments are subjective design choices, not scientific evidence.
  • The tool cannot detect trademarks, impersonation, account ownership, platform acceptance or actual availability.

Use a score as one input

Choose a name you can spell and say, avoid personal information such as a full birth date, and check it in the destination platform. A familiar name with a lower score may serve your audience better. Compare and Championship reuse this calculation; a tied bracket may need a tie-break.
