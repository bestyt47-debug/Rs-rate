how the score actually works

Every username gets scored across four categories, each out of 25 points, for a total out of 100:

Vibe looks at the overall feel of the username — does it read as confident, chaotic, soft, or edgy, and does that feel consistent from start to end instead of mixing three unrelated ideas together.

Originality checks how common the pattern is. Usernames built from a name plus a string of numbers (like alex2024) score lower here than something that couldn't have been generated by just mashing a keyboard.

Aura is the hardest to describe but you know it when you see it — it rewards usernames that sound like they belong to someone specific, rather than a placeholder account.

Memorability is about length, pronounceability, and spelling. A username you can say out loud once and remember beats one with three silent letters and a random underscore.

The scoring is rule-based, not AI-generated and not random — the same username will get the same score every time you check it. It's meant to be a fun, consistent gut-check, not a scientific verdict, which is why we're upfront that it's "judged by questionable science."

what actually makes a username good

Short beats clever. A username someone can type from memory after hearing it once will always outperform a longer one that's technically wittier. If you have to explain the pun, it's probably too long.

Numbers at the end usually mean the short version was taken. If your username ends in a birth year or a random "2," try a different word combination before falling back on numbers — most platforms still have four-letter-plus options free if you're flexible on the exact word.

Consistency across platforms matters more than most people think. Having the same (or close to the same) handle on Instagram, TikTok, Discord, and GitHub makes you easier to find and look more established, even as a new account.

Underscores and periods hurt memorability more than they help originality. They're easy to forget and easy to mistype when someone's searching for you later — reserve them for when the plain word is genuinely taken everywhere.

frequently asked questions

Is the rating a real algorithm or is it random?
It's rule-based — the site analyzes your username's structure (length, word patterns, number usage, character mix) and scores it against the same four criteria every time. Enter the same username twice, get the same score.

Does Rate My Username store or share my username?
No account is required to rate a username. If you choose to save a result or create an account to appear on the leaderboard, see our Privacy Policy for exactly what's stored.

Can I use this to pick a username for a specific platform?
Yes — the scoring criteria apply generally, but check the blog for platform-specific guides (Instagram, Discord, TikTok, Roblox, and more) if you want naming conventions and availability tips for that exact platform.

Why did my username get a low score?
Usually it's one of: heavy number usage, an overly long or hard-to-spell word, or a very generic word-plus-number pattern. Try the Compare tool against a tweaked version to see which change moves the score the most.
