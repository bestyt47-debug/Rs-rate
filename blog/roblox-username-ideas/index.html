Your Roblox username follows you into every game, friend list and chat. It's what other players see on the leaderboard, what friends search for when they want to team up, and what you'll be stuck with if you don't want to spend Robux on a change. That makes it worth a few minutes of thought before you press create.

Roblox also has some of the strictest rules on the internet, and for good reason. Usernames can't include spaces, real names or contact details, and they follow a set pattern of letters, numbers and at most one underscore. Here are 100 ideas that follow every rule, sorted by vibe. If you're a parent helping a kid pick one, skip to the tips below, since a safe name matters as much as a cool one.

How to pick a Roblox username safely

Roblox names are public and permanent-ish, so a few safety habits are just as important as style.

  • Never use personal info. Skip your real name, age, school, town or any contact details. Roblox blocks many of these anyway, and it's safer for everyone.
  • Know the format. Usernames are 3 to 20 characters using letters, numbers and at most one underscore, which can't be the first or last character.
  • Don't rely on numbers. A random string like Player84729 is forgettable. Use words you like instead.
  • Skip anything that copies other players. Names close to famous creators or other players can get flagged for impersonation.
  • Use your display name for extra style. Roblox display names have their own flexibility, so keep the username clean and put the fun there.

A quick note for parents

If you're picking this out with (or for) a kid, a couple of platform-specific safeguards are worth knowing:

  • Roblox already blocks a lot. The platform filters obvious personal info — real names, phone-number-shaped strings, common address patterns — but it can't catch everything, like a nickname only classmates would recognize.
  • Display names are separate and changeable. If a kid wants to express more personality, the display name (changeable more freely, with its own filter) is the safer place for that than the permanent-ish username.
  • Age verification affects some features. Certain chat and social features are gated by Roblox's age checks regardless of username choice — the name itself isn't a safety setting on its own.

FAQ

What are the Roblox username rules?
Usernames must be 3 to 20 characters and can contain letters, numbers and up to one underscore, which can't be the first or last character. They can't be all numbers, include spaces or contain personal information.
Can I change my Roblox username?
Yes, but it costs Robux, currently 1,000 at the time of writing. Because of the fee, it's worth choosing carefully at the start. The change is made in account settings.
What's the difference between a username and a display name?
Your username is your permanent login and unique ID. Your display name is what other players see first, has looser rules, and can be changed for free every so often. Both are public.
Can I use my real name on Roblox?
It's best not to. Roblox discourages sharing personal information, and a made-up name is safer, especially for younger players.
Will I lose my items or friends if I change my username?
No. Your items, Robux, friends and game progress stay with your account. Only the name changes, so tell friends so they can still find you.
Can I change my Roblox username for free?
Roblox gives you one free username change; after that, changes cost Robux. Display names, by contrast, can usually be changed more often and don't carry the same restrictions.
