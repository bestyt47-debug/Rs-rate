Steam is a slightly different beast from most platforms. There's no unique @ to fight over. Your public identity is a profile name that doesn't have to be unique, which means you can pick something creative without hunting for available variants. That freedom is also a trap, since anyone can share your name, so a good one needs personality to stand out.

Your name appears in your friends list, in every review you write, in trade chats, in workshop credits and next to your achievements. It's also easy to change, though Steam keeps a visible alias history, so past names aren't fully hidden. Below are 100 ideas across five styles, from cool and cryptic to cozy and collector-core. Use them as they are or as a base for your own.

Already set on Steam and second-guessing it? Check it with the rater before you rename yourself in front of your whole friends list.

How Steam names actually work

Steam separates several kinds of names, and knowing the difference saves confusion.

  • Profile name is public and flexible. It can be up to 32 characters, doesn't need to be unique and can include capitals and symbols.
  • Account name is private and permanent. This is the login name you chose at signup, and it can't be changed.
  • Custom URL is separate. You can claim a custom profile URL, and that one must be unique, so it's the place to fight for a clean handle.
  • Aliases are visible. Steam shows recent past profile names to friends, so don't assume a change wipes the slate.
  • Pick something you can say on voice chat. Friends will search for it, so avoid tricky symbols that are hard to type.

FAQ

How long can a Steam profile name be?
Your profile name can be up to 32 characters and can include letters, numbers, spaces, capitals and many symbols. It doesn't have to be unique, so other players can share it.
Can I change my Steam name?
Yes, as often as you like. Open your profile, click Edit Profile and update the Profile Name. Steam keeps a history of recent names that friends can see, so it isn't a fully clean slate.
What's the difference between a profile name and an account name?
The account name is the private login name you set at signup and can't change. The profile name is the public one shown across Steam, and it can be edited freely.
What is a Steam custom URL?
It's a unique address for your profile that you can set in Edit Profile. Unlike the profile name, it must be unique, so it's the best place to claim a clean handle you want to share.
Will changing my Steam name affect my library or friends?
No. Your games, achievements, friends and reviews stay attached to your account. Friends may take a moment to recognize you, so it helps to tell them.
Does Steam show my old profile names to other people?
Yes. Steam keeps a visible alias history that friends can see on your profile, so a rename doesn't fully erase past names — plan your choice with that in mind rather than assuming a clean slate.
