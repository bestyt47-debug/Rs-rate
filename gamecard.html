how GameCard works

You pick a platform (Gaming, YouTube, Instagram, Streaming, or Other) and a visual style for the card's background and color treatment. Your name and bio get laid out on a vertical poster sized for sharing straight to a story or profile — no design tools needed.

Stuck on a bio? The "Get OG Bio" shortcut generates one for you and drops it straight into the card, so you're not stuck staring at a blank text box.

when to use this instead of the other tools

The rating, roast, compare, and championship tools are about judging a username. GameCard is the one that's about presenting it — a quick, good-looking visual for your profile or a gaming clip, built directly from the @ you already rated.

picking a style that actually fits

The four styles aren’t just filters — they’re built for different platforms and different first impressions. Esports reads clean and competitive, which works well for a gaming profile, a tournament bracket entry, or a Discord server banner. Cyberpunk leans louder and more saturated — better for a TikTok or Twitch profile where you want something that stops a scroll.

Neon works well as a dark-mode-friendly banner since it’s built around contrast against black, and Pro is the one to reach for if the card is going somewhere more public-facing, like a gaming resume or a stream “about” page.

Since switching styles re-renders instantly with your existing text, the fastest way to decide is just clicking through all four once before downloading — it takes a few seconds and the right one is usually obvious once you see it.
