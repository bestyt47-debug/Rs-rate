how GameCard works

You pick a platform (Gaming, YouTube, Instagram, Streaming, or Other) and a visual style for the card's background and color treatment. Your name and bio get laid out on a vertical poster sized for sharing straight to a story or profile — no design tools needed.

Stuck on a bio? The "Get OG Bio" shortcut generates one for you and drops it straight into the card, so you're not stuck staring at a blank text box.

when to use this instead of the other tools

The rating, roast, compare, and championship tools are about judging a username. GameCard is the one that's about presenting it — a quick, good-looking visual for your profile or a gaming clip, built directly from the @ you already rated.

frequently asked questions

Is the poster free to download?
Yes — build it, preview it, and save it with no sign-up required.

Can I change the style after I've filled in my name and bio?
Yes, switching platform or style re-renders the poster with your existing text, so you can try a few looks before downloading.

What size is the poster?
It's a vertical 9:16 image, sized for sharing directly to a story, status, or profile banner.
